# 3% Company Research

3% Company Research

CV for Semester 4 Internship position

To start off, before you can create a *tailored CV* you need to know who you are tailoring it to!

Complete a Company Research Report on a Company of your choice. 

-  This report will be one page (not more than two pages max.)

**OBJECTIVE**

 To provide yourself with an understanding of the company in order to submit a Work Placement application

**REPORT GUIDELINES**

You may, but are not limited to, including details of:

1. Company Overview: 

A general overview of the company: name, location,  the number of staff, the
industry it operates in, its key competitors and so on.

2. Background of the Company: 

History and Evolution: Key milestones and growth of the company.

Mission, Vision, and Values: The company's guiding principles and long-
term goals.

Products and Services: description of the company's offerings

3. Skills Overview

Consider this company and outline what are the key skills that they would value with **internship** applicants from you, a student of the BSc in SETU:

a. list some (5-10) main soft skills that the company value in employees, and

b. list some (5-10) main hard/technical skills that the company value in employees.
