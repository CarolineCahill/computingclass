# Meeting Resources

Resources

You might choose to generate your meeting agenda, minutes and reflection on *any* topic of your choice

Examples:

Choose one of the following:
1. A webinar or podcast relevant to your topic
2. A recorded panel discussion (e.g. from Teagasc, Coillte, EPA, etc.)
3. A YouTube presentation (e.g. on urban forestry, biodiversity, crop trials) OR 
4. a written report or article that contains expert opinions

You’ll pretend this is the content of your “meeting”

It may relate to any topic, such as:
- Planning a forest restoration project
- Responding to an ash dieback outbreak
- Designing a new edible garden in a public space
- Applying for a climate-smart agriculture grant

**SAMPLE RESOURCES**

[Teagasc The Signpost Series Webinars](https://teagasc.ie/environment/climate-change-air-quality/the-signpost-series-webinars/)
- This is a sustainable agriculture / environment series

[Teagasc Horticulture Webinars](https://teagasc.ie/crops/horticulture/horticulture-webinars/)
- Teagasc runs a horticulture webinars series (covering mushrooms, nursery stock, fruit, plant health, etc.) through its Horticulture Development Department

[Watch events](https://organicgrowersireland.ie/resources/)
- Events & webinars for commercial growers eg from Commercial Horticulture Conference

[GIY Food Matters](https://giy.ie/blogs/food-matters-podcast/)
- GIY talks spuds, snacks and food security

[AHDB Horticulture and Potatoes website](https://horticulture.ahdb.org.uk/webinars)
- Agriculture and Horticulture Development Board online events and webinar archive

[Conversation Beneath The Trees](https://podcasts.apple.com/ie/podcast/conversation-beneath-the-trees/id1668557248)
- This Irish Agroforestry Forum podcast features episodes such as “The art of forest gardening” (with guests Andrew Ormerod, Donal Chambers, and a forestry inspector) and “Cattle, trees and the hills” discussing upland farming and agroforestry

[Pro Silva panel](https://prosilvaireland.com/videos/)
- Pro Silva Ireland has hosted panel‑style webinars such as “Forest Establishment Design for Continuous Cover Forestry” with multiple panellists

[Let's Talk Forestry](https://www.forestry.ie/videos-webinars)
- "Let's talk forestry, a forest owner's journey" takes you through the life cycle of sustainably managed forests in Ireland, in terms of economic, environmental and social sustainability, as told by private woodland owners.
