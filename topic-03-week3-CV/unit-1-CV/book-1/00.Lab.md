# 10% Placement CV

10% Placement CV 

For this lab, download & save any MS Word Resume template to your [OneDrive](https://onedrive.live.com/login/) 

The aim is to prepare you for your Semester 4 **placement opportunity** by having a one-page CV for potential placement employers to see

![CA Overview](./img/CAoverview.png)

You are to create a visually attractive one-page CV ready for a your BSc placement using a MS Word template of your choice

- This will count toward [10% of your CA](https://moodle.setu.ie/course/section.php?id=7440922).
